## What shipped

### Implementation (1 change, delta re-audited)

- `TAR_TVF_TYPE`: `/^([bcdhlps-])([rwxStTs-]{9})(?:[ \t]|$)/` → `/^([bcdhlps-])([rwxStTs-]{9})(?:[.+]?)(?:[ \t]|$)/`
- GNU tar appends `+` (ACL) or `.` (SELinux) after the 9 perms on systems with ACLs/SELinux. Old regex fail-closed aborted the whole listing, breaking legitimate GNU-tar exports. Suffix cannot enter type/perms captures; delimiter still mandatory.
- Stage 4 delta re-audit (Claude Opus 4.8): **Excellent, 0 blocking**. Style nit logged (redundant non-capturing wrapper).
